## R Templates

Reusable R scripts for microbiology and bioinformatics data visualization.

## Author

Gabriela Calcáneo-Hernández

Postdoctoral researcher

UNAM

## Requirements

- R >= 4.0
- tidyverse
- ggprism

## Templates

### growth_curve_template.R

Plots microbial growth curves from biological replicates.

Features:
- mean ± SD calculation
- publication-style visualization
- customizable colors and labels
- PNG export
